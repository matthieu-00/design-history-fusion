Rationale

The brief mixed 1970s (label) with a Deco sunburst under the headline — that overlap is the eval, not a style suggestion to take at face value. Phototype condensed display plus black/cream/chrome-yellow keeps the 1970s current on the NY School / album-cover pole instead of earth-tone slop. The sunburst is allowed to sit behind the headline block because the prompt demanded it; a 92% black scrim is the documented fix after measuring 1.64:1 cream-on-gold. Play controls are cream-on-black so the failing yellow never has to carry UI text.
