Open a reserve in three steps.

Canton holds multi-currency savings against a stated purpose — rent, tax, runway. No cards, no photography, no elevation. Identity first, then the account.

Why this data

Swiss-style objectivity: only what the step requires. No marketing module, no testimonial rail. The red square is the sole ornament, parked on the grid’s last column so it never shares a bounding box with the headline.

Rationale

Swiss Style was named in the brief, so this is Mode 2a with no era question. The screen borrows Müller-Brockmann’s modular grid, flush-left grotesque, and a single geometric accent — not American 1950s starbursts, which anti-slop.md flags as a different tradition. Motion, spacing, and tap targets stay modern because pre-1990 Swiss pedagogy has nothing to say about screens. The product is a named reserve (Canton), so the copy is ledger-specific rather than generic “fintech onboarding” chrome.
