Fusion Spec: Kindling dashboard

Anchor era: 2010s Material Design (Google I/O, 25 June 2014 — verified this session), not 2010s flat pastel-gradient SaaS.
Intensity: subtle nod (warm paper + elevation; no costume chrome).
Assumed, because the brief said “just try something”: low-stakes Mode 2a. “Warm and inviting” is read as Material’s paper/ink metaphor on a warm surface, not as a license for blob-and-card kitsch. Roboto (Christian Robertson, 2011, Android Ice Cream Sandwich; later Material’s recommended face) is the era-native UI type.
Frontend-design skill: available this session; used as the usability cross-check.

Rationale

The brief was low-stakes (“just try something”), so this proceeds in Mode 2a with the assumption stated: 2010s means Material’s elevation model, not the flat pastel-blob SaaS look anti-slop.md calls the decade’s default. Warmth comes from paper/ink surfaces and a forest primary, not from terracotta or gradient meshes. Roboto is cited as a 2011 screen face, which is why body type is allowed to stay in-era instead of being swapped for a later humanist.
